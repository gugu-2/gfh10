## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Realignment

### The Fundamental Problem with Current `trao.ai`
When an enterprise buyer (a CTO, COO, or VP of Engineering managing \$50M–\$1B+ P&L) lands on the current `trao.ai`, the site suffers from an **identity crisis**:
- **It markets like a seed-stage dev shop / freelance agency:** It promises *"MVPs in 8–12 weeks"*, quotes *"Typical AI SaaS projects start from £25,000"*, and offers *"growth through Meta Ads and Google Ads"*. No Fortune 500 or \$100M mid-market CTO hires an AI engineering lab that also runs Facebook ads.
- **It relies on toy gimmicks:** An intrusive floating widget with four AI persona buttons (Call Agent, Sales Agent, Live Agent, Chat Agent) creates visual noise and feels like an off-the-shelf wrapper demo rather than resilient infrastructure.
- **Typographic chaos:** The existing site loads **six different font families** simultaneously (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Jura`, `Poppins`, `Noto Sans`, `DM Sans`, `Instrument Serif`), signaling a lack of typographic discipline.
- **Assertions over evidence:** It uses standard industry buzzwords (*"cutting-edge"*, *"10x productivity"*, *"capabilities competitors won't have for years"*) without leading with the actual engineered architecture.

### The Realigned Value Proposition
Trao is **an embedded software and AI R&D lab**. They place senior engineers and systems designers directly inside enterprise teams to research broken workflows, rebuild them as production-grade AI systems, and deploy software that **survives contact with production at 3:00 AM on a Tuesday**.
